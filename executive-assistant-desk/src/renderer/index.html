Attention mode

For reminders that must not be missed: a loud alarm plays, then the reminder is spoken, a flashing alert appears on top of all windows, and it repeats until you press Acknowledge (or Snooze / Dismiss). The test plays the full sequence and repeats every 20 seconds, up to 3 times.

Use it for these meeting reminders

The alarm volume is relative to the Windows volume. “Mute” in the top bar silences the alarm and voice; the flashing alert still appears.

The meeting title is sent to this service. With ntfy.sh, anyone who knows the topic name can read it, so use a long, hard-to-guess name.

Spoken reminders

Meetings are announced 30, 20, 10 and 5 minutes before they start, together with a Windows notification.

Windows

Closing the window keeps the app running in the system tray (bottom-right of the taskbar) so reminders continue. To exit completely, right-click the tray icon and choose Quit.
